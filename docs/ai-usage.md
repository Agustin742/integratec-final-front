## Long hairstyles (3 shared styles) modular assets evidence

- **Tools and scope:** Antigravity and Blender MCP authored the 3 Long hairstyles from [asset wishlist](art/asset-wishlist.md) — `Long straight`, `Long wavy`, and `Long curls` ([guide](art/hair-long.md)) — as shared wearables under `assets/blender/avatars/shared/hair/` and `public/models/avatars/shared/` without modifying `male/body.glb`, `female/body.glb`, legacy `pants`/`boots`, or any exported top.
- **Useful result:** Built double-walled, back-tapered U-hem long hair masses reaching mid-back (`z ≈ 0.48–0.50`) weighted `100%` to `DEF-spine.006` above `z = 0.82` with smooth `DEF-spine.003` torso/shoulder blending below `z = 0.82` up to `0.50` at `z <= 0.56` (`Long straight` `71,152 B` / `9,602` verts; `Long wavy` `83,192 B` / `11,998` verts; `Long curls` `114,428 B` / `14,120` verts). All exports use neutral `#FFFFFF` `AvatarHairTint` (roughness `0.65`), canonical 34-bone rest matrices, and pass decoded GLB roundtrips with `0` eye, female eyelash, ear, and body/back/Wave-arm collisions (`p.z > 0.42`) across `Idle 1`, `Walk 62`, `Sit 123`, and `Wave 201` on both genders.
- **Detected problems and corrections:** Automated clearance checks and iterative Cycles preview inspections (`IsoFront`, `IsoRear`, `Front`, `Rear`, `Gray`) caught and resolved six defects before final export: (1) horizontal `Reference_Head` raycasts below `z = 0.87` hit the `0.036 m` neck stub instead of the wide skull (clamped raycast height to `z >= 0.87` and curved the lower curtain gently toward the torso back), (2) the initial rectangular `wy * wz` ear clearance envelope created boxy earmuff slabs (replaced with a smooth asymmetric Gaussian ellipsoid around `(±0.345, -0.010, 0.886)`), (3) discrete `if` Z-cutoffs in `push_point_clear` caused subtle silhouette zig-zags (replaced with continuous $C^1$ deficit blending), (4) the single-centroid end-cap triangle fan folded over the front temple edge (replaced with clean row-by-row pillowed end-cap quads), (5) overlapping `top_blend` with `Cap`'s lower rim caused a grazing sawtooth seam at `z = 0.96–1.04` (shifted `top_blend` strictly inside `Cap` above `z = 1.03`), and (6) initial `Long curls` had a top-pole pinwheel crease, spaced bang pills, and an abrupt mid-back ringlet cutoff (smoothed the crown pole, overlapped the curly fringe, and staggered ringlet roots up into the crown).
- **Human verification:** Approved by human visual review of the Blender review scenes (`REVIEW_HairLong{Straight,Wavy,Curls}_{Male,Female}` in `odd/previews/long-hair-review.blend`) and permanent `1280×1280` / `360×360` renders (`docs/assets/hair-long-*/`); committed locally on `feat/91-modular-avatar-assets` (not pushed; runtime catalog integration and wishlist merge-check remain separate).
